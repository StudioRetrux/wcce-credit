What it is

A wall of light that watches you move.

  1. 01

    You step in

    Walk up to the wall. No app, no controller.

  2. 02

    A camera reads you

    Body tracking finds where you stand and how you move.

  3. 03

    The wall answers

    3D blades, drawn live, rise and turn to follow you.

Why we made it

A machine can make an image. Only you can make a moment.

WCCE 2026, the 5th World Conference on Creative Economy, believes creativity still begins in a person. So we built a piece that does nothing on its own. Hundreds of blades sleep until someone steps in, then the whole field turns to follow them.

  1. Your body is the brush

    No buttons. Just move.

  2. A world you walk into

    Realm means stepping into another world.

  3. Better together

    One person ripples it. A crowd makes it bloom.

How it works

It wakes. Then it blooms.

Act I

The Realm wakes

Walk past and the field stirs, as if it had been waiting for you.

Act II

Everyone, together

Enough people, and all four pillars bloom into the WCCE 2026 mark.

Then

It rests

The field settles back to sleep, ready for the next crowd.

Why Realm

One of four pillars.

WCCE 2026 stands on four pillars around one centre, the Human. This piece is built from Realm: stepping fully into another world.
